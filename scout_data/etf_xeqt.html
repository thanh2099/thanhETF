How it's taxed

How your distributions are taxed

The CRA taxes each distribution dollar by its character. These shares are measured against the full taxable distribution, including any portion the fund reinvested as units rather than paying in cash.

Character (2025)% of taxable$ / unit

Foreign withholding tax already taken inside the fund: $0.0485 per unit. In a non-registered account, foreign tax reported on the T3 may be claimable as a foreign tax credit, subject to T2209 limits. TFSA and RRSP accounts do not claim that T3 credit.

$0.3221 per unit was reinvested as additional units rather than paid in cash. It is still part of the taxable distribution above, so it is taxed in 2025 even though no cash reached your account, and it appears on your T3 with no matching deposit.

Return of capital lowers your adjusted cost base, so the deferred tax can increase a later capital gain or reduce a later capital loss when you sell. Its tax classification alone does not identify its economic source.
