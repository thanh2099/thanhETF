Fund structure

What this fund is

Holds covered-call ETFs that sell call options on U.S. stocks for income, and has 1.25x leverage. Returns come from the underlying equity basket plus option income, with some upside typically traded away for higher cash distributions. The fund borrows money to increase its exposure, which amplified both gains and losses and added borrowing costs.

How it's constructed

HYLD.TO holds covered-call ETFs instead of writing the options itself.

The covered-call ETFs it holds write the options, and their premiums help fund the payout. Those funds are Canadian-listed, so there is no extra US-listing layer; foreign tax on the underlying stocks is still withheld at the fund level.

How it's taxed

How your distributions are taxed

The CRA taxes each distribution dollar by its character. These shares are measured against the full taxable distribution, including any portion the fund reinvested as units rather than paying in cash.

Share of the distribution, %

Character (2025)% of taxable$ / unit
Return of capital100.0%$1.7680

Return of capital lowers your adjusted cost base, so the deferred tax can increase a later capital gain or reduce a later capital loss when you sell. Its tax classification alone does not identify its economic source. If return of capital keeps building up and your cost base reaches zero, any further return of capital is taxed as a capital gain in the year you receive it, not deferred to a sale.
