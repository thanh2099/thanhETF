Do I connect my brokerage?

No. You record your buys and sales, and the Lab does the rest: it picks up each distribution your funds pay, including reinvested ones, and adds it for you automatically.

Which accounts can I track?

TFSA, RRSP, FHSA, RESP, RRIF and non-registered accounts, each on its own or all together.

What happens after the trial?

Your first charge is on day 8, $9 CAD a month or $60 a year. Cancel before then and you won’t be charged.

Is this investment advice?

No. DecodeETF is an information service, not a registered adviser. The Lab describes your own portfolio; it doesn’t tell you what to buy or sell.
